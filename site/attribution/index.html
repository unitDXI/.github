Licensing and attribution

You are free to reuse Unit 511 material: in a class, a company training, a blog post or a course of your own. The one requirement is that you give us credit.

What is licensed how#

Licences for Unit 511 material
MaterialLicenceWhat it requires
Lessons, course pages, blog posts, notebooks’ text, images and diagramsCC BY 4.0Credit Unit 511, link the licence, and say if you changed anything
Code samples, notebook code, and the site’s HTML, CSS and JavaScriptMITKeep the copyright and licence notice in copies

Both licences allow commercial use and modification. The Unit 511 and Fairytale.ai names and logos are not covered by these licences. Please don’t use them in a way that suggests we endorse your work.

How to credit us#

Every lesson and course page ends with a ready-made attribution and a Copy attribution button. The general form is:

“Title” by Unit 511 (Fairytale.ai and Unit 511 contributors), page URL, licensed CC BY 4.0.

If you changed the material, add what you changed, for example “adapted: examples translated to R”.

In a course built on ours

Put a credits page or section at the start of the course. List each Unit 511 lesson you used, with its title and URL, the licence, and a summary of your changes. Your own additions can use any licence you choose. Our parts stay CC BY 4.0.
