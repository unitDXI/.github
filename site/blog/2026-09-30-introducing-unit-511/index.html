Introducing Unit 511

30 September 2026 · Unit 511 team

announcements curriculum

Today we are opening the doors of Unit 511, Fairytale.ai’s open-source training, experimentation and development group. This site is where our curriculum lives. It is free and public, and it is licensed so that anyone can reuse it with credit.

Why another curriculum?#

There are plenty of places to watch someone explain AI. There are fewer places where you build the pieces yourself and publish what you make. Unit 511 is built around that difference. Every course ends in an artifact: a notebook, a tool, a library or a merged open-source contribution. Every lesson moves that artifact forward.

We also wanted material that doesn’t lock learners into one vendor. Examples run on free, public tools, and whenever a lesson names a product, it names alternatives too.

What’s in the first stage#

The curriculum starts with language and tool tracks (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, SQL, Python, Java, R, C, C++ and Git, coming soon) and continues with AI Engineering: sixteen courses in four stages. We are publishing its first stage, Foundations & Tooling, first:

  • 511-101 Introduction to AI Systems: tokens, embeddings, generation, retrieval and agents, from first principles.
  • 511-102 Python for AI Engineering: the everyday Python behind AI tooling, with tests.
  • 511-103 Git, Open Source & Contribution: how to land your first real pull request.
  • 511-104 Math for Machine Learning: vectors, probability and gradients, in code.

The first lesson is already live: Tokens and Embeddings. You build a byte-pair-encoding tokenizer and a similarity search from scratch, and you can run the code right in your browser. More lessons will follow over the coming weeks.

How to take part#

  • Learn: start any published lesson now. No sign-up needed.
  • Join a cohort: apply with a short form. Cohort dates will be announced here and in GitHub Discussions.
  • Contribute: fix a typo, propose a lesson or volunteer as a mentor. See how to contribute.

We’re looking forward to building in the open with you.
