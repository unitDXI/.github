Code of Conduct

Unit 511 exists so that anyone can learn to build in the open. That only works if every participant can take part without harassment or exclusion.

Where this applies#

This code applies to all Unit 511 spaces. That includes our GitHub repositories, issues, pull requests and Discussions; cohort sessions and chats; mentoring conversations; and events where you represent Unit 511. It applies to learners, mentors, maintainers and visitors alike.

What we expect#

  • Be welcoming and patient, especially with beginners. Everyone here is learning something.
  • Critique work, not people. Make review comments specific, kind and actionable.
  • Assume good intent, and ask before you assume.
  • Credit other people’s work, and respect licences and maintainers’ time.
  • Respect privacy. Don’t share other people’s personal information or private messages without permission.

What is not acceptable#

  • Harassment, intimidation or discrimination of any kind, including on the basis of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, religion, nationality, experience level or appearance.
  • Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
  • Personal attacks, insults, trolling and deliberately derailing discussions.
  • Publishing someone else’s private information without explicit permission.
  • Using Unit 511 spaces to spam, advertise, or pressure other projects with low-effort contributions.
  • Any other conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional learning community.

Reporting a problem#

If you experience or see behaviour that breaks this code, please tell a maintainer privately through the contact route listed on the unitDXI GitHub organisation. If a maintainer is involved, contact a different one. Reports are kept confidential and shared only with the people who need to act on them.

What happens next#

Maintainers will review every report and respond as quickly as they can. Depending on what happened, the response may be:

  1. A private conversation, with an explanation of why the behaviour was a problem.
  2. A formal warning, with clear consequences if it continues.
  3. A temporary suspension from some or all Unit 511 spaces, including cohorts.
  4. A permanent ban, for serious or repeated violations.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits or other contributions that break this code. Anyone subject to an enforcement decision may ask for it to be reviewed by maintainers who were not involved.

Credits#

This code was written for Unit 511 and is inspired by the Contributor Covenant, which is widely used across open-source projects.
