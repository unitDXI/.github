Learn by building. Ship in the open.

Unit 511 is Fairytale.ai’s open-source training, experimentation and development group. Our learning tracks are free and public. They cover the languages and tools every developer needs (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, SQL, Python, Java, R, C, C++ and Git) and go all the way to building AI systems. Every course ends with something real you build and share.

Start learning Contribute

What Unit 511 is

Tracks and cohorts

Learn a language or tool at your own pace, then move on to AI Engineering, four stages from foundations to leadership. Learn alone at any time, or join a cohort.

Artifact-driven

Each course builds one thing: a notebook, a tool, a library or a merged contribution. You leave with work you can show, not just a certificate.

Open to everyone

Every lesson is free, public and licensed CC BY 4.0. Examples run on free, public tools, with no paid API keys and no vendor lock-in.
