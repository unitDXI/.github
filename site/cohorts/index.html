Join a cohort

Every lesson is open to learn on your own. A cohort adds people: a group working through the same AI Engineering stage together, with a mentor and peer review of what you build.

Apply for the next cohort

It takes about five minutes. The form opens on Google Forms.

Open the application form

Free · No payment details · Hosted by Google Forms

What a cohort is#

  • One stage, together. A cohort works through one stage of AI Engineering, such as Foundations & Tooling, on a shared schedule.
  • Regular sessions. Members meet to work through lessons, ask questions and show progress.
  • A mentor. Each small group has a mentor who reviews artifacts and helps with first contributions.
  • Peer review. Everyone reviews someone else’s artifact. Giving feedback is part of the curriculum.
  • Free and open. There is no fee, and all the material stays public, whether or not you join.

Who can join#

Anyone who wants to learn by building. Each stage lists its prerequisites on its course pages. For the first stage you only need some programming experience in any language. Each cohort announces its dates and the weekly time it expects when applications open.

How to join#

  1. Read the Code of Conduct. Everyone in a cohort agrees to it.
  2. Fill in the application form: who you are, which stage you want to join, your experience, and how much time you can give.
  3. Maintainers review applications when the application window closes, and email everyone who applied with the outcome and next steps.
  4. If you’re accepted, you’ll get the schedule, your group and your mentor’s name before the first session.

Your data#

The application form is hosted by Google Forms, so Google’s privacy policy also applies. Your answers are seen only by the Unit 511 maintainers who run cohorts. They are used only to organise the cohort, and they are deleted after it ends. The form asks only for what we need, and you won’t be added to a mailing list.

If you can’t or would rather not use Google Forms, start a private conversation with a maintainer through GitHub Discussions and we’ll arrange another way to apply.

Not ready to join?#

Start any published lesson now, with no sign-up needed. New cohorts are announced on the blog (RSS) and in Discussions.
