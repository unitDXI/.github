# Security policy

This policy is the default for every repository in the `unitDXI` organisation.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report privately through **GitHub's private vulnerability reporting**: [open a draft security advisory](https://github.com/unitDXI/.github/security/advisories/new) on this repository, or on the affected repository if it has its own. **Don't open a public issue for a security problem.**

Include what you found, where, how to reproduce it, and the impact you expect. We aim to acknowledge reports within five working days.

## Reporting content that could harm readers

If a lesson, notebook or tool could lead someone to leak secrets, run untrusted code unsafely, or damage their own or someone else's system, report it the same way and we'll treat it as a security issue. Examples: a sample that asks readers to paste an API key into a public notebook, or instructions that disable a safety check without explaining the risk.

## Scope

In scope:

- The curriculum site at `unitdxi.fairytale.ai`, including its in-browser "Try it" sandbox
- This landing page at `unitdxi.github.io/.github`
- Public repositories in the `unitDXI` organisation

Out of scope:

- Third-party tools, libraries and models named in lessons. Report those to their own projects.
- Denial-of-service testing, and testing of GitHub, Google Forms or other platforms we use. Report those to the platform.
- Social engineering of Unit 511 members or Fairytale.ai staff.

## Safe harbour

We won't pursue or support legal action against good-faith research that stays within this scope, avoids privacy violations and service disruption, and gives us reasonable time to fix the issue before public disclosure.
