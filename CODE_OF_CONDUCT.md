# Code of Conduct

Everyone who takes part in Unit 511 and the `unitDXI` repositories agrees to the Unit 511 Code of Conduct. It covers issues, pull requests, Discussions, cohort sessions and mentoring.

**Read the full code:** https://unit511.fairytale.ai/site/code-of-conduct/
(source: [`site/code-of-conduct/index.html`](site/code-of-conduct/index.html))

In short:

- Be welcoming and patient; everyone here is learning.
- Critique work, not people.
- Credit other people's work and respect maintainers' time.
- Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks and sharing private information are not acceptable.

To report a problem, contact a maintainer privately. Reports are kept confidential.
